Glaucoma: Silent Cause of Blindness

Glaucoma is the leading cause of irreversible blindness in Japan among adults 40+.

Caused by optic nerve damage from high intraocular pressure (IOP — the fluid pressure inside the eye).

Progresses silently until late stages.

Early prediction is key to prevention.

3% of adults over 40 affected globally
Glaucoma prevalence increases with age, affecting 1 in 33 adults worldwide

Model for Predicting Glaucoma Progression

Structure: Age → IOP → Optic Nerve Damage → Visual Field Loss.

Captures causal links between risk factors and vision loss.

Populated with hospital data (age, IOP readings, imaging, vision tests) and expert priors.

Estimates the probability of vision loss within 12 months to support clinical decisions.
