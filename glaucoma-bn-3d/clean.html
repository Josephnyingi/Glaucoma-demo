Glaucoma: Silent Cause of Blindness

Glaucoma is the leading cause of irreversible blindness in Japan among adults 40+.

Caused by optic nerve damage from high intraocular pressure (IOP — the fluid pressure inside the eye).

Progresses silently until late stages.

Early prediction is key to preventing permanent vision loss.
